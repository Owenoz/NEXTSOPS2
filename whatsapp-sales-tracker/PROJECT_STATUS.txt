================================================================================
COMMAND REFERENCE
================================================================================

AddProduct <name> <qty>
  Example: AddProduct Sugar 100
  Creates new product with initial stock

Restock <name> <qty>
  Example: Restock Rice 50
  Adds quantity to existing product

Sold <name> <qty> <price>
  Example: Sold Sugar 5 2000
  Records sale and deducts from stock

Stock
  Lists all products and their quantities

Summary
  Shows daily revenue total

Undo
  Restores last sale and returns stock

Help / MyShop
  Shows available commands
